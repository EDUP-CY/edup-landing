About Project Spark and EDUP.CY

The edup.cy platform was founded in September 2026 in Limassol by an information technology professional committed to making digital tools and information accessible in Cyprus, through short step-by-step guides created for independent learning. Our current focus centers on Project Spark: a workshop pilot series running through November and December 2026. Following this experimental validation phase, refined digital guides and self-paced learning modules will be published here.

What the platform prepares to offer:

  • Practical lessons: Short, introductory modules focused on practical skills like banking basics and safety, online commerce and utility management, public portals, and travel.
  • Mobile-friendly design: Content built to be read on your mobile phone during breaks or outside of working hours.
  • Free for all: All learning materials are completely free, open to the public, and easy to share.
  • Community support: An independent community initiative dedicated to education and support.
